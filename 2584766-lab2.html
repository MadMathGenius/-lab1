About Me

Hi there! I am Dakalo Hope Ramulongo, a third year student at the University of the Witwatersrand, majoring in Computer Science and Mathematics. I have always been interested in tech stuff, and have been haunted with the curiosity of how digital things work. Studying Computer Science at Wits has caused me to fall in love with Mathematics, to explore it's roots and possibly advance it. I was raised in a small township in South Africa, Pretoria called Mamelodi. My goal and wish is to use my knowledge of Math and Computer Science to advance the livelihood of the people in my community, my contry, and possibly the rest of the world.

Latest Project

The latest tech project I worked on was with my friends here at Wits for the Hackathon competition. The name of our project is donorDash, a platform where individuals, food stores, resturants, and other institutions could offer access food to the less priviledged and homeless. The website was to be user-friendly, where individuals who donate would stand a chance to win coupons for free meals at the food stores and resturants we would be associated with.
